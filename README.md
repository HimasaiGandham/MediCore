# 🌍 MediCore — Multilingual Medical Support
> **Branch:** `feature/multilingual`  
> **Role in MediCore:** Helping users ask and understand medical guidance in multiple languages.

---

### 🌟 What is this branch all about?
Health questions shouldn't be limited by language barriers! Many patients, students, and community health workers speak languages other than English 🌍. This branch is dedicated to **Multilingual Support** — allowing MediCore to understand and answer in various languages.

---

### 💡 Why do we need this in MediCore?
If a community health worker in a rural area asks a question in Spanish, Hindi, or French:
- The system should translate the clinical question into English to search the authoritative guidelines 🔍.
- Retrieve the verified medical evidence from WHO or CDC 📄.
- Translate the final verified answer back into the user's native language with clear explanations! 🌐

---

### ⚙️ How does it work in simple words?
1. **Detect Language:** Recognizes the language of the user's question 🗣️.
2. **Cross-Language Search:** Maps the query to the English medical guidelines without losing meaning 🔄.
3. **Localized Response:** Presents the verified answer and safety disclaimers in the user's preferred language 💬.

---

### 🤝 How this branch contributes to MediCore
- 🌐 **Global Accessibility:** Makes trusted healthcare information accessible worldwide.
- 🤝 **Community Health Empowerment:** Helps non-English speaking healthcare workers consult global protocols.
- 🛡️ **Preserves Accuracy:** Ensures translations stay true to official clinical guidelines.

---

### 🦄 What makes this branch unique?
This branch breaks down **language barriers in medicine**, enabling global accessibility while maintaining strict grounding in authoritative guidelines.

---

### ⚠️ Important Health Reminder
MediCore is an educational and reference assistant. It does not replace the judgment of a licensed doctor. Always call your local emergency service in critical situations! 🚑

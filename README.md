# 📋 MediCore — Structured Clinical Response Formatting
> **Branch:** `feature/response-formatting`  
> **Role in MediCore:** Turning long walls of text into clean, easy-to-read clinical summaries.

---

### 🌟 What is this branch all about?
Nobody likes reading a giant, unbroken wall of 500 words — especially when looking for urgent medical guidance! This branch provides **Response Formatting** 📋 — organizing answers into clean bullet points, bold takeaways, and structured sections.

---

### 💡 Why do we need this in MediCore?
A busy clinician or student needs to absorb key information in 5 seconds:
- **Bad:** A single huge paragraph mixing definitions, symptoms, emergency warnings, and source citations together 😫.
- **Good:**  
  - 📌 **Key Takeaway:** Immediate high-level answer  
  - 🩺 **Clinical Guidance:** Clean bulleted instructions  
  - ⚠️ **Precautions:** Highlighted warnings  
  - 📑 **Source Reference:** Clear citations at the bottom ✨

---

### ⚙️ How does it work in simple words?
1. **Template Parsing:** Structures the AI's generated response into standardized sections 📐.
2. **Visual Hierarchy:** Uses markdown headers, bullet points, callout boxes, and bold text for easy reading 🎨.
3. **Summary Highlights:** Extracts an executive summary at the top of each answer 💡.

---

### 🤝 How this branch contributes to MediCore
- 👁️ **Instant Readability:** Users find critical points without hunting through paragraphs.
- ⚠️ **Clear Safety Highlights:** Crucial warnings and disclaimers are impossible to miss.
- 🎓 **Professional Clinical Layout:** Answers look clean, organized, and authoritative.

---

### 🦄 What makes this branch unique?
This branch is the **editorial designer of MediCore**. It ensures that high-quality medical evidence is presented with clean readability.

---

### ⚠️ Important Health Reminder
MediCore is an educational and reference assistant. It does not replace the judgment of a licensed doctor. Always call your local emergency service in critical situations! 🚑

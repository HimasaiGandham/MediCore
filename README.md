# 🔐 MediCore — User & Staff Authentication
> **Branch:** `feature/authentication`  
> **Role in MediCore:** Keeping medical data safe and managing who can log in.

---

### 🌟 What is this branch all about?
In healthcare, patient safety and privacy come first! This branch is dedicated to **User Authentication** 🛡️. It makes sure that doctors, researchers, medical students, and everyday users can safely log into MediCore with proper permissions.

---

### 💡 Why do we need this in MediCore?
Think of MediCore like a hospital library:
- A **student** might just want to read general health facts 📚.
- A **doctor** might need to review deep emergency clinical guidelines 🩺.
- An **administrator** needs to keep everything secure 🔑.

Without authentication, anyone could access or change anything. This branch creates the digital door with keys and badges!

---

### ⚙️ How does it work in simple words?
1. **User Sign-In:** A user enters their username and secure password 🚪.
2. **Badge Check:** The system verifies who they are and gives them the right permissions 🏷️.
3. **Safe Access:** The user can now safely consult medical guidelines tailored to their role 🏥.

---

### 🤝 How this branch contributes to MediCore
- 🔒 **Data Protection:** Keeps health queries private.
- 👨‍⚕️ **Role Management:** Separates general public access from certified clinician tools.
- 📜 **Accountability:** Helps track who asked what for research and safety logs.

---

### 🦄 What makes this branch unique?
This branch is built as a **standalone security module**. It operates independently on its own feature track so that login and security logic do not interfere with medical search or AI answer generation.

---

### ⚠️ Important Health Reminder
MediCore is an educational and reference assistant. It does not replace the judgment of a licensed doctor. Always call your local emergency service in critical situations! 🚑

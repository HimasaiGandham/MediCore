# ⚠️ MediCore — Patient Safety & Emergency Guardrails
> **Branch:** `feature/safety-layer`  
> **Role in MediCore:** Protecting patient health with prominent disclaimers and emergency alerts.

---

### 🌟 What is this branch all about?
In any healthcare technology, **safety is the #1 priority** 🛡️. This branch implements MediCore's **Safety Layer** — displaying clear medical disclaimers, recognizing emergency keywords, and reminding users that MediCore is an informational assistant, not a doctor.

---

### 💡 Why do we need this in MediCore?
If someone searches for *"severe chest pressure and radiating pain in left arm,"* they might be having a heart attack right now 🚨!
- The system must immediately urge them to call emergency services (**911, 112, or 999**).
- It must clearly state that this software cannot diagnose or treat illnesses.
- It must keep users safe by preventing dangerous self-medication decisions.

---

### ⚙️ How does it work in simple words?
1. **Disclaimer Banners:** Displays a permanent clinical disclaimer in the app header and sidebar 🚩.
2. **Emergency Keyword Detector:** Detects critical terms like "chest pain," "anaphylaxis," "unconscious," and "stroke" ⚠️.
3. **Urgent Action Prompts:** Displays prominent emergency alert cards recommending immediate medical attention 🚑.

---

### 🤝 How this branch contributes to MediCore
- 🛡️ **Protects Human Life:** Ensures emergency situations are directed to real-world healthcare providers.
- ⚖️ **Ethical & Legal Compliance:** Complies with healthcare AI safety standards.
- 🩺 **Promotes Responsible Use:** Keeps users focused on research, education, and reference.

---

### 🦄 What makes this branch unique?
This branch is the **ethical and safety conscience of MediCore**. It builds the critical guardrails that keep human health protected.

---

### ⚠️ Important Health Reminder
MediCore is an educational and reference assistant. It does not replace the judgment of a licensed doctor. Always call your local emergency service in critical situations! 🚑
